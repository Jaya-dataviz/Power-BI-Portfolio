Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard

This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales performance, profit margins, and discount impact across regions and segments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aim

- Understand sales trends and profitability
- Identify high-performing regions and products
- Support leadership in data-driven decision-making
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tools Utilized

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Insights

- Sales grew 249% compared to last year
- Government segment contributed the highest revenue
- Midmarket delivered the highest profit margin (27.6%)
