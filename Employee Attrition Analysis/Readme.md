#Employee Attrition Analysis
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition across tenure, compensation and satisfaction dimensions to identify key drivers such as over time, salary slab and work life balance.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aim:

The aim of employee attrition analysis is to understand why employees leave an organization and how those departures affect business performance. 

It helps HR and management make data‑driven decisions to improve retention and workforce stability.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tools Utilized:

Microsoft PowerBI

Power Query

DAX
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Metrics:

Attrition Rate (%)

Average Tenure (years)

Attrition Count

Attrition by Salary Slab

Attrition by Work‑Life Balance Score
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Insights & Findings

“Employees with low work‑life balance scores had 2x higher attrition.”

“Attrition was higher for those earning below 5K.”
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Business Impact:

How HR teams could use this dashboard:

Identify high‑risk groups.

Design retention strategies.

Reduce recruitment costs.
